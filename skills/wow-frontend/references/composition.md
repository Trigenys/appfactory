# Composition — make the landing worth scrolling

The sequence must follow the buyer's real decision, not a list of widgets.

## Establish one visual thesis

State who the page is for, the pain solved, the primary CTA and the product behavior to demonstrate. Choose one coherent art direction and two honest alternatives. Specify:

- typography: headline/body pairing and scale;
- layout: container width, grid, vertical rhythm, visual asymmetry;
- palette: existing tokens first; one clear accent and high contrast;
- visuals: self-hosted real assets or clearly labeled mockups; mobile crop;
- interaction: intentional proof of product behavior;
- sections: why each exists and what visitor learns;
- mobile hierarchy: what survives without animation and hover.

## Example story skeleton (adapt, never blindly paste)

1. **Hero:** one product promise, one primary CTA, clear visual product evidence.
2. **Product in context:** show recognizable work in the actual product UI.
3. **Progressive transformation:** a user action leads to an observable outcome.
4. **Relevant capabilities:** three differentiated jobs, not twelve vague features.
5. **Truthful trust:** security/process facts, authorized real examples or none.
6. **Clear conversion:** CTA to the actual destination and a short expectation.

Bento grids, sticky storytelling, large editorial typography and clean browser/phone mockups are **tools**, not requirements. Avoid same-looking AI SaaS gradients, empty decorative blocks and infinite scrollytelling.

## Commerce Factory example — real repo observed

`Trigenys/trigenys-commerce-factory` is React 19/TypeScript/Vite with existing plain `src/styles.css`, FR/EN content, optimized self-hosted AVIF/WebP imagery, storefront/product visuals and WhatsApp handoff. It should **not** be migrated to Tailwind, Next.js or Webflow for a landing refresh.

A grounded story is:

```text
merchant product imagery
 → catalog/storefront presentation
 → product selected
 → WhatsApp action with product context
 → practical merchant benefit
```

Preserve actual app links, FCFA context and the bilingual interface. Fictional demonstration stores must be labeled demos. No unapproved prospect branding, fake merchant results or made-up testimonials.

The reference brief in `skills/wow-frontend/examples/commerce-factory.brief.json` is for **read-only planning**; it does not change Commerce Factory.
